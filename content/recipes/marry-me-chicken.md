Marry Me Chicken is one of my favorite foods but raw chicken is annoying to work with, so here's a 20-minute version with all the flavor and none of the pain, served over rice instead of meat.

* 1/2 T. butter or olive oil
* 2+ cloves garlic, minced
* Salt, pepper, thyme, and crushed red pepper flakes to taste
* 1 C. low-sodium chicken broth
* Additional chicken bouillon (say, 1/2 T. Better Than Bouillon or a couple cubes of the dry stuff)
* A small handful of sun-dried tomatoes, diced
* A few glugs of heavy cream
* A handful of parmesan cheese
* 1/2 tsp. sodium citrate (optional, for a smoother sauce)
* Cooked white rice

1. Warm up butter/oil in a saucepan over low heat.
2. Add garlic, salt, pepper, thyme, and red pepper. Warm for a minute or two; do not brown the garlic.
3. Add chicken broth, bouillon, and sun-dried tomatoes. Raise heat to medium and simmer for a few minutes.
4. Add cream and parmesan. Heat, stirring occasionally, until the cheese is melted.
5. Add sodium citrate, if using. Stir to combine.
6. Serve over rice.
